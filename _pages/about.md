Kevin is an embedded software and hardware engineer, with a particular interest in the design and execution of interactive systems.

Kevin graduated from California Polytechnic State University, Pomona, with a B.S. in Computer Engineering in 2020. From there, he worked at Northrop Grumman as an Associate Software Engineer in various departments, before moving to Beaudry Interactive as a Creative Technologist, then Lead Embedded Systems Engineer.

His work primarily revolves around end-to-end embedded project development, from requirement collection, hardware designs, firmware development, physical assembly, and project installation. With strong experience in both well-defined and R&D type projects

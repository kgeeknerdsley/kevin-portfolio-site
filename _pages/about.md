Hi! I am a Lead Embedded Systems Engineer working in various software/hardware areas for Beaudry Interactive, focused primarily in interactive electronics, computer vision, aerospace applications, and R&D processes.

I have background in both electrical design and software development, excelling in projects where the two fields collide. My industry experience comes primarily from themed entertainment and the aerospace fields. I also have held various leadership roles on projects, possessing not only technical knowledge but organizational skills involving both people and things.

I spend my time across all aspects of a project, from requirement definition, initial prototyping, full production of both hardware PCBs and firmware, through the installation and commissioning of the project. My experience in each area allows me to drive projects from beginning to end, making me a valuable contributor at any stage of the process.

I graduated from California Polytechnic State University, Pomona, with a B.S. in Computer Engineering in 2020.

I have research and personal interests in the fields of computer vision, machine learning, embedded development, and application of embedded systems to the aerospace and themed entertainment industries.

## Overview
This project was developed for the Engineers without Borders student chapter at Cal Poly Pomona, as part of the Research and Development team. 

The subteam of four that I led was tasked with designing a low-cost and low power sensor that could indicate the purity of water at any stage of a modular water filter design, ensuring each stage's functionality, and detecting any problems before the water would be consumed. The sensor prototype utilized an Arduino, which measured the water's conductivity, turbidity, and temperature to provide an estimate of the water's purity.

I was responsible for designing the system, selecting the microcontroller and components, as well as providing a prototype and generating reports as to the device's success. 

I also managed a team of four, mentoring and instructing non-ECE students as well as delegating work based on interests and strengths.

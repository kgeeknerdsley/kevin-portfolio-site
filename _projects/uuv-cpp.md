## Overview
This project was developed as a part of the Northrop Grumman Collaboration Project, a project spanning both Cal Poly Pomona and Cal Poly San Luis Obispo's campuses. Multiple teams from each university collaborated to create a series of autonomous search-and-rescue vehicles. 

The vehicle was designed to execute a search and rescue style mission, with an end effector able to grasp objects. The vehicle boasted dual cameras, and was able to utilize computer vision to detect an orange buoy, then autonomously navigate to the buoy. It also had a ballast system onboard, permitting it to rise and sink as needed.

I was responsible for writing foundational code in C++ for the autonomous navigation, as well as assisting in the design and assembly of the vehicle's internal electrical systems. I also contributed to the design of the target buoy, using computer vision principles to influence design and material decisions.

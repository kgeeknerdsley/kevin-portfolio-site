## Overview
Augmented Putting is a part of [Atomic Golf](https://thestrat.com/attractions/atomic-golf) in Las Vegas, NV. I worked on this as the Lead Embedded Systems Engineer at [Beaudry Interactive](https://www.binteractive.com/post/augmented-putting).

The Augmented Putting project is a gamified putting experience, where golf balls on the field are tracked using a tightly integrated series of cameras and computer vision detectors, who all work together to produce an accurate and responsive map of balls, which is passed to a game engine to integrate into gameplay. The system covers a large field, and provides persistent, environment-tolerant real-time tracking in changing outdoor conditions.
